01 — The idea

A logarithmic spiral

Time doesn't repeat — it rhymes. The mark is a true logarithmic spiral: the same self-similar curve as a nautilus shell, a galaxy, a hurricane. It looks like itself at every scale, so the story it tells at 16 pixels is the story it tells on a billboard.

Each dot is a moment. The arm winds outward from a vanishing point in deep time, growing but never breaking its rhythm — cycles that widen without ever losing their shape.
